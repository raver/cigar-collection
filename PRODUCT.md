# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: people who grew up in 80s China, nostalgic for that era's textures and memories. They arrive curious, stay for recognition — "I remember this one."

Secondary: developers and peers who notice the craft. They arrive for the tech stack, stay for the feeling.

## Product Purpose

A personal museum of vintage Chinese cigarette labels — hand-picked, hand-photographed, presented with warmth. Not a database, not a marketplace, not a collector's index. The site exists so these small artifacts are seen by someone, not left to yellow in a cardboard box. Success is a visitor leaving with a felt sense of 文艺, 怀旧, 感伤 — the quiet ache of a remembered time.

## Positioning

A personal museum of vintage Chinese cigarette labels — intimate as memory, comprehensive as archive. 100+ labels today, room for 1,000+.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: browse the gallery (浏览全部收藏)
- Secondary CTA: leave a message on the guestbook (留言板) — not per-label, but for the site as a whole
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: 往事如烟，岁月盲盒。80年代童年时光的美好，值得我们通过烟标这样的小物件来重温。
- Belief ladder:
  1. These are real, personally collected artifacts — not stock photos, not a database dump.
  2. The time spent browsing will feel meaningful, not empty.
- Proof on hand: the artifact images themselves are the proof. Each label is photographed by the collector, showing wear, texture, and age.

## Brand Personality

文艺、怀旧、感伤、温暖. The tone is personal and quiet — like someone opening a worn photo album, not a curator delivering a lecture. Reference aesthetic: the film poster for "黄金时代" (The Golden Era, 2014) — ink-wash textures on paper, gold lettering, white space as breathing room, a sense of something beautiful that has passed.

## Anti-references

- Flashy e-commerce: hero carousels, urgency badges, "trending" labels
- Cold minimalism: Swiss-design grids, monospace typography, concrete-gray everything
- Tech-bro SaaS: gradient CTAs, metric dashboards, "modern" as the only adjective
- Academic museum catalog: sterile white walls, tiny labels, emotional distance
- Anything that feels generated, templated, or algorithmically optimized at the expense of soul

## Design Principles

- **真实胜于完美** (Authenticity over perfection): every label shows its age — worn edges, faded ink, fold marks. These are not defects; they are the story. No artificial "cleanup" of the artifacts.
- **节制胜于堆砌** (Restraint over excess): the site is a frame, not a performance. No parallax scroll, no hover explosions, no interaction for interaction's sake. Let the labels speak.
- **时间的质感** (Texture of time): the visual language should feel tactile — paper grain, ink bleed, gold patina — not slick digital gloss. Materials over effects.
- **温暖不煽情** (Warmth without sentimentality): nostalgic, yes. Maudlin, no. The warmth comes from recognition, not manipulation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG target or known user needs at this time. The day/night theme toggle and reduced-motion alternatives for animations are the current baseline.
